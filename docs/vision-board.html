Chariot

The harness of harnesses. Keep every agent you love. Chariot makes them one team.

Every agent is a harnessed horse. Chariot makes them pull one load. You hold the reins.

Your agents stop wiping each other's work. "Done" means verified done. A subscription that runs out hands the task over instead of stalling. Anything can join: a CLI, an SDK agent, a local model, a website.

The promise

Five things no single harness or workbench carries.

No clobbering

Reservations on every resource, held edits, and revert detection, across every vendor's agent.

Done means done

An independent check re-tests every task against the final state. Agent summaries never decide.

Never stalls on quota

When a subscription runs out, the task moves to another member with its context.

Anything joins

CLIs, SDKs, local models, APIs, terminal apps, websites. An onboarding agent writes the driver.

A god model that decides

It routes, splits and resolves on its own. Every decision is logged and explainable, and only five kinds of question ever reach you.

Principles

The rules Chariot never breaks.

  • No credentials in the core. Every member logs in through its own harness.
  • Subscriptions are used only through their official doors, at a human pace.
  • The god model decides, and every decision can be explained with chariot why.
  • Only five kinds of question reach you. Irreversible actions always ask.
  • One writer per resource. Reviewers come from a different model.
  • Local first. One machine, one SQLite file, free to run.
  • The interface is beautiful and fast, because you live in it all day.
Risks

What could go wrong, and the answer already in the design.

RiskAnswer
T3 or Zed add locks and verificationDepth and evidence on the trust layer, auto-onboarding nobody else has, and the up-link so Chariot works under their app too.
Vendors flag automated useOfficial surfaces only, human pace, no token handling.
Vendors break driversDrivers are data, re-certified on every version change and repaired by the onboarding agent.
Handoffs lose the reasoningHandoff packs carry decisions and why. One writer per resource. Reviewers get a clean context.
The god model makes a bad callEvery call is logged and explainable, the acceptance check catches the damage, and five hold types put you in the loop.
Shell commands bypass the gateSnapshot and diff around every command.
